How to play Gate Sync (free browser game)

English guide · no download · Practical AI Lab

Goal

Gate Sync is a short, one-thumb timing puzzle. A light travels forward along colored rails. Your only job is to switch which rail the light rides so it passes through every matching color gate and reaches the finish line. There is no endless runner scroll, no character to dress up, and no install step — open the page and play.

Controls

Tap or click the left half of the play area to move to the previous rail, or the right half to move to the next rail. On a desktop keyboard, Space (and Right Arrow) cycles forward; Left Arrow cycles backward. The light never stops on its own. Portrait mode on a phone (around 390×844) is the intended layout; landscape still works with the canvas centered.

Colors, gates, walls, and gaps

Each solid gate sits on one rail and shows a color. When the light reaches that gate’s row, you must already be on that rail — that is the “match.” If you are on a different rail, the level soft-fails and you retry from the start of the same level. Walls block a single rail: ride into one and you fail. Blank (dashed) segments are safe gaps — any rail is fine. Use gaps to recover timing before the next colored gate.

Levels in v1

Level 1 · Warm-up uses two rails and six matching gates at a calm speed. It teaches the tap-to-switch habit with no walls and no gaps.

Level 2 · Split Path opens a third rail, raises speed by about twenty percent, and mixes ten gates with safe gaps and walls you must dodge early.

Level 3 · Twin Gates keeps three rails and adds stacked twin color gates. About 0.9 seconds before arrival an amber hint names the target color (for example “Aim AMBER”). Be on that rail when the twin hits. There is no separate lock tap. A bar above the twin shrinks as the window runs out. Speed rises another fifteen percent. Stars appear only after you clear Level 3: three for zero misses on that run, two for two or fewer misses, one for a clear with more misses.

Fail and retry

Wrong color, wall hit, or a missed twin window restarts the current level only. Attempt counts stay local for your own sense of progress; Gate Sync v1 does not publish leaderboards or fake rankings. Mute is available in the HUD for quiet spaces such as transit.

Sound

Mobile browsers block audio until you interact. Press Play to unlock Web Audio. All effects are tiny oscillator and noise blips generated in the page. No external music files.

FAQ

Is Gate Sync free?
Yes. Play Gate Sync in the browser with no download and no in-page purchase flow in v1.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no login, cloud save, or account wall.
Why is there no app store link?
Gate Sync is a static web game meant for hosting on GitHub Pages (or any static host). Keeping it as a web page avoids store fees and keeps the zero-spend lab constraint.
Can I play offline?
After the first successful load, your browser cache may let you reopen the files, but v1 does not promise a full offline Progressive Web App experience.
Why did sound not start?
Phone and desktop browsers silence audio until a user gesture. Press Play once to unlock sound, then use Mute if you prefer silence.
Is this related to Pulse Lane or similarly named games?
No. Gate Sync is an original timing and rail puzzle from Practical AI Lab. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a fan game of any product using pulse-and-lane branding or other banned lookalikes. We use the words light, rail, and gate on purpose.
How many levels are there?
At least three in v1 (Warm-up, Split Path, Twin Gates). More may unlock in later lab drops.
Who made this?
Practical AI Lab open game lab. See the About page and the hub placeholder linked from the footer.
Found a bug?
Note what level and device you used, then send a short report through the contact placeholder on the lab hub when it is published.
